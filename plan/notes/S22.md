# S22 — `main.rs` keeps the frame loop and gives up its chrome

Research behind the split of the shell's chrome into four modules and the shared test helper. It
stays after the commit because it holds the measured seam, which is what the next shell split (U45)
reads before moving the scene-bound state onto `Document`.

## What was measured

- `apps/furnicraft/src/main.rs` at `5748a95`: 3467 lines, 2608 production and 859 test. The tail
  between `home_surface` and `impl eframe::App` was 632 lines — the number the S7 audit misread as
  `home_surface`'s length (see `plan/notes/S21.md`).
- After the move: `main.rs` 2037 production and 265 test; `status_bar.rs` 287; `side_panels.rs`
  159; `strip.rs` 114; `shortcuts.rs` 82; `test_support.rs` 30. The 71 extra lines over the 571
  moved production lines are the five module headers and their imports.
- The suite is unchanged (T1, B8): the same 34 test names in the same bodies, spread over
  `main.rs` (14), `strip.rs` (9), `status_bar.rs` (5), `shortcuts.rs` (5) and `side_panels.rs` (1);
  `test_support::drawn_text` is the one helper three of them share. `cargo test -p furnicraft`
  passes 226 tests.

## Where the seam was drawn

- `rule` sits in `side_panels.rs`, not in `status_bar.rs` as the task guessed: its only caller is
  `side_panel`, so keeping it with the bar would have made a one-line function into a second
  contract.
- `ribbon_band` and `ribbon_inset` stay on `FurniCraftApp`: they read the measured band and the
  editor's open state, so they are shell state, while `strip.rs` holds only the geometry that takes
  its arguments.
- `viewport_owns_view`, `preview_nodes`, `is_under_any`, `selection_outline`, `preview_vertex_mesh`
  and `apply_viewport_defaults` stay in `main.rs`: they are neither chrome nor band, and U45 takes
  them onto `Document`.
- The seam is `pub(crate)`: `status_bar.rs` adds one inherent `impl FurniCraftApp` for the bar's
  three methods, and `PanelHead`, `Shortcuts` and the moved functions are visible to the shell only.

## Ruled out

- Moving `scene_tree`, `panel_card` and `ribbon` into the modules: they hand a body of document widgets
  to the panel and the strip, which is the composition the shell keeps, and B8 says a move changes no
  logic, not that it relocates the most callers.
- A `mod chrome` with four submodules: the four pieces change for four different reasons (a report,
  a fold, a band, a key), which is exactly what B2 asks a module header to say.

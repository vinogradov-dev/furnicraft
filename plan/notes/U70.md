# U70 — Icons are coloured by semantic role

Icons are coloured by semantic role

## Role table

| family | dark ink | light ink | used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| draw | #58C8DC | #1C7C92 | create/modify sketch geometry (U69) |
| modify | #A98BF0 | #6B4BB0 | transform and edit commands |
| model | #6E9EF7 | #2E5CB8 | solids, bodies, booleans |
| scene | #55C7A4 | #237A5E | panels, hardware, cameras, lights |
| view | #B8C2CE | #5A6674 | navigation, projection, display |
| danger | #E06A6A | #B33A3A | destructive commands only |

The accent (#E0A24A / #A6620F) is UI chrome and must never be a role ink: an active tool is a
background and a border, not a recoloured glyph.

## Pipeline

- A master may use at most three inks from this table, at the existing tonal levels
  (`fill-opacity` 0.12–0.18 passive, 0.45–0.60 result); one ink may be the result colour.
- `IconFamily` lives on the icon (not on the theme directory): `contour/arc` is draw,
  `actions/delete` is danger, `view/frame-all` is view.
- `RoleRamp` maps each role hex to the theme's ink before the texture is uploaded, so one master
  serves dark and light; the mapping is a six-entry table per theme.
- The mono path is the alpha silhouette multiplied by the theme icon colour; it is the automatic
  fallback below 20 px and the `Never` mode.
- The cache key is `(uri, px, theme, mode)`; a theme or mode change re-rasterises, a widget state
  change only remultiplies.
- Disabled = alpha multiply; hover/press = one lighten step of the mapped ink (8–12 %), not a
  second table.
- Contrast: each role ink against `panel` and `band` ≥ 3:1 in both themes; the draw ink is the
  weakest on P2 and is the one to check first.

## Why 20 px

At 16 px a two-tone fill reads as a muddy blob and the outline loses its weight (AUDIT.md §2);
at 20 px the three tones separate. The threshold is a pixel count, not a point size, so a 0.75 UI
scale falls back automatically.

## Tests

- every preset × theme renders the expected role colour in the pixels;
- the alpha silhouette is identical dark vs light;
- mono at 16 px equals the tint path;
- every hex in every SVG is in the table;
- no icon has more than three inks;
- each role clears 3:1 on both themes;
- a mode switch re-rasterises once per `(uri, px)`.

## Ruled out

- One asset set per theme or per palette: the palette changes neutrals, not icon meaning.
- CSS variables, masks, gradients or a colour token language inside the SVG: the rasteriser does
  not resolve them, and the guide forbids the elements.
- Using the UI accent as a role ink: active state would disappear among the icons.
- Four inks: three faces and one outline is the most that survives at 22 px.
## What the session built (2026-11-12)

- `crates/ui/src/icons.rs` gained `Family` (the six role families), `RoleRamp` (DARK is the master
  table, LIGHT the paper column), `IconTone` and the substitution pass. A master keeps writing the
  dark literal; `role_svg` replaces each literal before `load_svg_bytes_with_size`, so the alpha
  silhouette is the drawing and dark/light differ only in RGB (asserted). A file that is not UTF-8
  falls back to the one-ink path instead of failing.
- The family is a macro argument per icon: the theme names its default (`contour` → draw,
  `primitives`/`bodies` → model, `scene` → scene, `view`/`window` → view, `actions` → view) and an
  icon overrides it where meaning and surface disagree. `undo`, `redo`, `add`, `duplicate`, `rename`,
  `copy`, `paste`, `visible`, `hidden`, `lock`, `unlock` are `modify`; `delete` and `remove` are
  `danger`. `error`, `warning` and `info` stay `view`: the note's table says danger is for destructive
  commands, and the status shape already carries the meaning.
- The 80 drawn files were re-inked mechanically (`#fff` → the family literal) and their geometry did
  not move: the footprint/coverage test passes on the same measurements. `primitives/cone` stays the
  one zero-byte placeholder.
- Threshold: `settings::ICON_COLOR_MIN_PX = 20` and `IconColor::uses_roles(px)`. `Auto` uses the role
  inks at 20 px and above, `Always` at every size, `Never` never. The mode is published into `egui`
  context data (`furnicraft_ui::set_icon_color` / `icon_color`) like the language, and the shell
  writes it once per frame.
- Tone: `Rest`, `Hover`/`Press` (one step 10 % away from the surface — white on dark, black on paper),
  `Disabled` (the resting role texture multiplied by `visuals.disabled_alpha`) and `Selected` (the
  theme foreground, because the accent fill is chrome). The cache is `(uri, px, variant, lightened)`
  for a role texture and `(uri, px, tint)` for a one-ink one; Rest and Disabled share a texture and a
  second frame uploads nothing.
- Measured contrast of the six inks against `panel` and the ribbon `surface` over three presets ×
  two variants: 4.30 : 1 … 9.57 : 1 (weakest: light `draw` on the P1 ribbon; the test floor is 3 : 1).
- Visual check: a throwaway contact sheet of all 80 icons in both variants, then the running app on
  the dark theme — the ribbon's Sketch group reads cyan, the scene glyphs green, undo/redo purple,
  delete red, the viewport cluster neutral, and no glyph lost its shape. The sheet was rendered at
  32 px from the same substitution code and deleted with the review.
- `assets/README.md` (§ role inks and the format), `STYLE_GUIDE.md` §1.1/§5/§6/§7.5/§15/§19.4,
  `AUDIT.md` §1/§5/§8, the icon skill and ADR 0090 carry the new rule; 0047 and 0055 are marked as
  superseded on the one-ink clause only.

## Ruled out

- A ramp per preset: the preset changes neutrals and the accent, not meaning, and a preset switch
  would re-rasterise every icon for no visible difference.
- A role ink under a chosen toggle: the ink on the accent fill loses contrast and the accent would
  become a glyph colour, which the note forbids.
- Baking the disabled alpha into the texture: an alpha multiply at paint time reuses the resting
  texture and is what the guide already promised.
- A second asset per theme or per state: the substitution runs per raster, and hover/disabled are
  tone steps of one texture family.

# S15 — the scene crate's camera and ray

Research behind the split that moved the camera and the ray casts out of `crates/scene/src/lib.rs`.
It stays after the commit because "why the camera kept its public surface" outlives the task; the
outcome is in `plan/BACKLOG.md`.

## What was measured

- `crates/scene/src/lib.rs` was 1276 production lines and 914 test lines. The production part held
  the camera family (lines 23–587), the ray and its casts (589–639, 1157–1275) and the frame
  (grid, lights, contact shadows, drawables, `SceneFrame`) in one file — over B4's 1000-line ceiling
  on its own and 82 public items over B6's 25.
- `Camera` carried ~30 public methods on one `impl` (orbit, look, roll, project, nav) — inside B4's
  800-line `impl` ceiling, and one value family, so it moved whole rather than being cut.
- The `ray_*` casts were already `fn`-private except `ray_segment` and `ray_triangle`; only
  `SceneFrame::pick` called them, and only `camera.ray_ndc` built a `Ray` outside the tests.
- The 914 test lines are one `mod tests` at the crate root using `super::*`; they touch
  `camera.near` / `camera.far` directly to build clip-plane checks and use `FRAC_PI_2` / `TAU`.

## Decisions

- `camera.rs` (584) keeps one type's surface: `Camera`, `Projection`, `CameraError`, `FixedBasis`,
  the fov/pitch/distance constants, `POLE_TOLERANCE`, `canonical_up` and `NDC_LIMIT`. The header
  states the B6 exception: 39 public items that are all questions about the same value.
- `ray.rs` (207) holds `Ray`, the pick radius, both hit helpers and the two cast primitives. The
  two helpers became `pub(crate)`, the `Ray` fields too, so `SceneFrame::pick` and
  `Camera::ray_ndc` keep the exact code they had; making them private to a module would have
  rewritten logic inside a move (B8).
- `lib.rs` stays the frame's module (570) and re-exports every moved name, so the shell, `render`
  and the crate's own tests keep saying `furnicraft_scene::Camera`. The header states the B6
  exception for the frame's value types.
- The tests did not move (T2 is optional and never mixed into a move): they gained one `use` line
  for the two `f64` constants that used to arrive through the crate root import.

## Ruled out

- Moving `pick` itself into `ray.rs`: it orders a whole frame (drawables, polylines, the camera of
  the frame) and needs `SceneFrame`'s fields, so it belongs to the frame; the casts are what the ray
  module can answer alone.
- Moving the camera tests into `camera.rs` beside their module: that is a test-layout change (T2),
  a second concern, and it would hide from the next reader which tests the move broke.
- A `Ray::unchecked` constructor for the `ray_ndc` fallback: the only caller is one line that
  already has a finite, unit direction, and the fields are `pub(crate)` for it.
- Putting `NDC_LIMIT` in `ray.rs`: it bounds the picking ray a *camera* generates, and no ray cast
  reads it.

# S17 — the vertex builders get their own module

Research behind the split that took the builders out of `render/lib.rs`. It stays after the commit
because "what stayed behind, and why" is the part S18 and the next render change inherit; the outcome
is in `plan/BACKLOG.md`.

## What was measured

- `crates/render/src/lib.rs` was 1136 production lines and 435 test lines at `2c183d4` (S16). The
  block from `mesh_centre` to `sanitize_color` was 351 lines whose only callers were
  `ViewportRenderer::upload`, `render_views` and the crate's one test module.
- Six constants, two types and ten functions travel with the builders: `MAX_VERTICES`,
  `MAX_GRID_DIVISIONS`, `SHADOW_SEGMENTS`, `MAX_CONTACT_SHADOWS`, `EDGE_COLOR_DARK/LIGHT`,
  `MeshVertex`, `EdgeSlice` and the ten functions the entry lists.
- After the move `lib.rs` is 742 production lines and `geometry.rs` is 433; `impl ViewportRenderer`
  stayed at 421 — over B4's 400 target, under its 800 ceiling, so no debt line and no split.
- The longest function in the new module is `edge_geometry` at 47 lines (B4's ceiling is 150).
- The tests did not move; the assertions are unchanged and the same 31 pass (T1).

## Decisions

- `MeshVertex` and `EdgeSlice` moved with the builders. S16 left `MeshVertex` in `lib.rs` on the
  promise that S17 decides: it is the builders' output type and the vertex format, so it belongs in
  the module that builds it, and `pipelines.rs`/`gpu.rs` read it from there.
- `sanitize_color` moved too: four of its five call sites are builders, and `lib.rs` imports it for
  the one viewport clear colour it still owns.
- The four budgets moved: only the builders read them. `MAX_VIEWS` stayed, because the camera
  uniform and `render_views` are what cap the view count.
- `stencil_slot` reaches `MAX_STENCIL_OWNERS` through `crate::pipelines`: two modules read one
  constant, so neither keeps a copy (C7).
- `shadow_uniform` and `point_uniforms` stayed in `lib.rs`: they pack the camera uniform the
  renderer writes, which is not vertex data.
- The test module gained two `use` lines — `crate::geometry` for the moved constants and
  `furnicraft_geom`/`furnicraft_scene` for the types its fixtures build — because `super::*` no
  longer reaches them through the crate root.
- The `lib.rs` header now says what it does not do: it builds no vertex, `geometry.rs` does.

## Ruled out

- Splitting the new module further (meshes / lines / shadows): each builder is 3–47 lines and they
  share `MeshVertex`, `push_line` and the vertex budget, so three modules would repeat one header
  and one import list for no reader's gain.
- Moving the test module beside the module under test (T2): it is optional and never rides in a move
  commit.
- Making the builders `pub`: nothing outside the crate draws a vertex, so `pub(crate)` keeps the
  surface honest — 19 crate items in the module, under B6's 25-item ISP line.
- Taking `RenderError` with the builders: it is the crate's refusal type for uploads too, and its
  doc comment belongs next to `upload`, which returns it.

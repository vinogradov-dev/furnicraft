# U73 — The corner widget takes the free corner the panels leave, below the ribbon band.

The corner widget takes the free corner the panels leave, below the ribbon band.

## What was measured

- `gizmo::widget_rect(viewport)` placed a 124 × 148 pt rectangle at `viewport.right - MARGIN`, and
  `Document::viewport` fed it `uncovered(rect, chrome.ribbon)`: the leaf rectangle below the band, with
  no knowledge of either panel. With the inspector visible the cube sat at the top-right corner of the
  view, which the inspector covers (U72 made the panels a true overlay and left this clearance to U73).
- `strip::uncovered` had one caller (the widget) and returned its rectangle unchanged in every case:
  `Layout::regions` tiles `Chrome::content`, which already starts below the band.
- **The inspector was wider than `PanelRects` said.** `PanelRects::of` gave 226 pt
  (1457.3..1683.3 at a 1691.3 pt view), but the card's rows demand 355.2 pt, so egui's `Area` grew to
  that width and was constrained left to 1328 — the panel was painted 129 pt left of its own rect.
  `Chrome::covers` therefore missed that strip: a press there went to the scene although a panel was
  drawn over it, and any widget placed left of 1457 still sat on the panel. Measured with `eprintln!`
  of `ui.min_rect()` per section: the widest rows are the name field beside *Rename* (304.7) and the
  property rows (355.2), not the long `Origin (local to …)` header (192.8).
- A live run at 150 % scale confirmed it: the inspector's fill and its header started at ~1328 pt while
  `chrome.panels.right` said 1457.

## Decisions

- `widget_rect(view, &Chrome)` owns the whole clearance: the band is cut first, then each top corner is
  measured against the panel on its side (`PanelRects::of_side`), right corner first, left corner as the
  mirror, and `None` when neither fits. `corner_rect` is the pure per-corner step.
- The widget is skipped, not hidden, when the display switch is off: `widget_placement(&cluster, …)`
  returns `None` and `Gizmo::show` takes the placement as an argument, so a drag's state survives a
  hidden frame and no click is consumed.
- `strip::uncovered` was removed with its test: the band clip moved into `widget_rect`, which now sees
  the ribbon, so a caller cannot forget it.
- `side_panels::scroll_body` wraps a panel body in `ScrollArea::both`, which bounds the body to the
  panel's inner width. This is what makes the inspector's painted rectangle equal `PanelRects` again;
  without it the panel is 129 pt wider than the shell believes, and neither picking nor the widget can
  be correct. The inspector scrolls sideways when its rows are wider than 226 pt (filed in
  `plan/debt.md`: the rows should reflow instead).

## Ruled out

- Placing the widget against the drawn panel rect: the panel's width depends on the card's content and
  is known a frame late, so the widget would chase it; the rectangle is the contract, so the panel was
  made to keep it.
- Widening the inspector to 355 pt: on the 640 pt minimum window that leaves 59 pt of scene beside the
  226 pt tree.
- Keeping `uncovered` beside the new placement: a second function for one band clip is a second truth.
- A hover-only switch or a second checkbox row: the popover already lists the display switches, and the
  readout's own switch stays independent of the cube's.

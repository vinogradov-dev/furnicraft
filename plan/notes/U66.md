# U66 — Floating panels, opacity and the chrome buttons

Floating panels, opacity and the chrome buttons

## Geometry

- The viewport occupies the whole area under the header and the ribbon; the panels are overlays.
- Panel width 226 expanded, 34 collapsed; inset 8 from the viewport edges; radius 8.
- Fill = `panel` at `opacity` (55–100, default 92); the header row is `surface` at the same alpha.
- Top = ribbon band + 8, where the band is the animated ribbon height (`open` 72, folded 29).
- Bottom = status bar top − 8; the status row stays outside the panels.
- The floating view cluster keeps `Order::Middle` and `constrain_to(viewport)`; the panels and the
  cluster must not overlap at the default size (checked at 1024×700).

## Picking and layout contract

- The shell receives `covered: Vec<Rect>` (both panels, the ribbon band, the status bar) once per
  frame and hands it to `scene_gizmo`/`contour_gizmo`/`view_layout`; a hit inside `covered` is not a
  viewport hit.
- The selection rectangle (drag) starts only outside the covered rects.
- The split-view tree tiles the scene rectangle, which is the viewport below the ribbon band; the
  panels float over it and narrow nothing (corrected by U72 — this note first said "the uncovered
  viewport", which made a fold reflow every view). The borders and their hit bands still work.
- The view cluster's own area is not covered by the panels; its clicks never fall through.

## Buttons

- In the tab row, right side: collapse all, hide all, fold ribbon. The tab row survives a fold,
  so the buttons are always reachable.
- Collapse all: both panels go to their 34 px strips; the prior expanded/folded state is stored so
  a second press restores it exactly.
- Hide all: both panels disappear (width 0); the prior state is stored the same way.
- Chevrons: neutral rectangle + divider + chevron toward the edge the panel folds to; the strip
  shows the mirrored glyph. Tooltips «Свернуть панель» / «Развернуть панель».
- Shortcuts `Ctrl+0` and `Ctrl+Shift+0`; verify they are free in `shortcuts.rs`; if not, pick and
  record the pair in the task note before coding.

## Animation

- `ctx.animate_value_with_time(id, target, 0.15)` for the ribbon band; the panels read the same
  value, so their tops never jump. The bottom edge does not move.
- The fold of a single panel animates its width with the same duration; the strip appears at the end.

## Settings

- `appearance.panel_opacity: u8` (55–100, default 92), used by the ribbon, both panels and the
  clusters (the cluster alpha is `opacity` clamped to 80–100 so it stays readable).
- `panels.scene: PanelState { visible, expanded }`, `panels.inspector: ...`; `#[serde(default)]`,
  unknown values fall back to visible+expanded.
- A Panels page in Settings: visibility, side (left/right fixed for now), opacity, reset. Level B
  (user panels and order) is not part of this task.

## Tests

- covered-rect hit test for the scene gizmo, the contour gizmo and the selection rectangle;
- hide-all/restore and collapse-all/restore are exact;
- opacity round-trip and a repaint assertion on the three surfaces;
- `panel_top(ribbon_height, folded)` is a pure function with a golden at 72/29;
- the split layout tiles the uncovered area and its borders remain grabbable;
- no document revision from any panel gesture.

## Ruled out

- Docked opaque panels (M1) as the default: the owner chose M2.
- A blur behind the panels: egui cannot blur the wgpu pass; the alpha is the effect.
- Per-panel opacity: one setting for the whole chrome.
- Hiding the ribbon band with the panels: the tab row is the way back and must stay.

## Correction — U72

The panels are a true overlay. `Chrome::content` returns the central area below the ribbon band and
ignores both panel rectangles, so folding, hiding, collapsing or restoring a panel leaves
`Layout::regions` and every camera aspect exactly as they were; the image was already painted over
the whole central area, which is what lets a 55 % panel show the furniture under it. `Chrome::covers`
is unchanged, so a press on a panel is still chrome. The cost is that `Frame all` now fits the view
below the strip only: the panels no longer narrow the fit and the user does not lose scene width to
them. The orientation widget was kept clear of the panels by the old narrowed rectangle; U73 places
it explicitly.

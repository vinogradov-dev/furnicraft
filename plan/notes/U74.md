# U74 — A tool with variants is a split button

A tool with variants is a split button: the main face runs the pinned method, a joined caret face
opens the list, and pinning changes the main face without covering a shortcut.

## What was measured

- U67 made the whole 34 × 30 cell open the list, so the method the workspace pinned needed two clicks
  (cell, then row) and the glyph never said which method a click would run. The cell was one
  `icons::ribbon_cell` response with a 5 × 2.5 pt wedge painted at `right - 6, bottom - 5`.
- The ribbon glyph is `RIBBON_ICON_POINT_SIZE` = **22** pt inside a 34 × 30 cell: 6 pt of air on each
  side, so any split line in that cell crosses the glyph's bounding box. The wedge already sits under
  the glyph's lower-right corner and paints no ink over it (`contour/circle`: ink ends at 24.6 pt of
  34; the wedge starts at 25.5).
- The list row was a right-to-left `Ui` flow: `pin`, `shortcut`, `6`, `glyph + 8`, label. A row
  without a key shifted the label right, the pin only existed while it was drawn (so the shortcut
  moved when it appeared), and a long name ran into the key because nothing truncated it. `actions/pin`
  is a role-ink asset, so at a 12 pt raster it takes the one-ink path and honours the caller's colour,
  but at 200 % (24 px) the role path would repaint it unless the tone is `Selected`.
- `ready()` already returned `armed` per method, and `method_rows` threw it away; the split cell needs
  it for the pinned method, which is not necessarily `tool.run`.

## Decisions

- `ribbon_split_cell` in `icons.rs` owns the two faces: the right `RIBBON_CARET_WIDTH` = 10 pt column
  is the caret, everything else is the method. Both are `ui.interact`ed over one `allocate_exact_size`,
  so the faces tile the cell and a pointer anywhere reaches one of them; the caret rect is 10 × 30, a
  target a hand can reach. The main face stays interactive when the method cannot run (guarded where
  the action is pushed) and the caret is always live, so a pinned method that cannot run never traps
  the person in it.
- The fill follows the face under the pointer, with the cell's corner radius on the outer edge and a
  square corner on the seam, so a hover shows the split without drawing a divider at rest. Armed is the
  tool's state, not the pointer's: it covers the whole cell.
- The glyph stays centred in the **cell**, not in the main face. Centring it in a 24 pt face leaves
  1 pt of air around a 22 pt drawing, and every split cell in the row would sit off the pitch its
  neighbours keep. The wash edge under the glyph's right arc (≈1 pt of stroke) is the lesser cost.
- `main_face()` is the one place that answers what the face is: the pinned row's method, its glyph or
  the tool's when the method has none, its label and key as the hint, its readiness and its action.
  The hint naming the method rather than the tool is the point of the gesture: «По 3 точкам (Shift+C)»
  says what the click does.
- The list row is four rects from `columns(rect, shortcut_width)`: the key column is measured once for
  the whole list from its widest key, the pin column is the pin's 20 × 12 control, and the name column
  is what is left, cut with an ellipsis (`LayoutJob` + `TextWrapping { max_rows: 1, overflow_character:
  '…' }`) instead of reaching the key. Nothing moves when the pin appears.
- The pin is drawn only on the hot row — the pointer over the row (read with
  `ui.rect_contains_pointer`, so the pin is a control inside a row that stays hot) or the keyboard's
  row — and it is only interactive while drawn, so an invisible pin cannot swallow the click that runs
  the method. The pinned row keeps its accent bar and fill as the persistent mark: that is what says
  «main method» when the pin is hidden. The pinned pin is `IconTone::Selected` with the accent, because
  the role ink would win at 200 %; an unpinned one takes the hover tone.
- Wording moved with the gesture: `PinMethod` → «Сделать основным способом» / "Use this as the main
  method", `MethodPinned` → «Основной способ» / "The main method", and the new `ToolMethods` →
  «Все способы» / "All methods" is the caret's name.
- The list is anchored to the main face (same left and bottom as the cell), so the split moves no
  pixels of the popup.

## Ruled out

- A divider between the faces: the task says no gap, and the hover already says where one ends.
- Centring the glyph in the main face, or shrinking it to 16 pt for split cells: both break the one
  pitch (ADR 0067, 0091) that makes a row of cells read as a row.
- Moving the wedge to the vertical centre of the caret face: the corner wedge is the existing
  convention and keeps the ink clear of the glyph.
- A separate `action/caret` asset: the wedge is the cell's anatomy, like the open tab's underline
  (U67).
- Opening the list on a main-face click while the caret exists, or letting the caret run the method:
  the two faces have exactly one job each; a main-face click while the list is open runs the pinned
  method and closes it, which is the answer the list was asking for.
- Changing the initial keyboard row: it stays row 0 (U67), so an untouched list shows one pin on the
  pinned row, which is also the row the accent marks.

## What the session built (2026-11-12)

- `icons.rs` grew `RIBBON_CARET_WIDTH`, `SplitCell { main, caret }`, `ribbon_split_cell` and
  `paint_split_cell`/`split_fill`; the wedge painting moved here from `toolbars::caret`, which is gone.
- `toolbars.rs`: `show_tool` splits into the one-method path (unchanged) and the split path that draws
  `main_face`, runs the pinned action on a main-face click and toggles the list on a caret click;
  `MainFace`/`main_face` are new; `method_rows` now carries `armed`.
- `list.rs`: `Columns`/`columns`/`shortcut_column` lay the four columns out once; `method_row` paints
  the pin conditionally and interacts with it only while drawn; `paint_row`/`label_galley` paint the
  glyph, the truncated name, the key and the pin; `ROW_INSET` and `PIN_COLUMN` are `pub(super)` for the
  layout test.
- Tests: main face runs exactly the pinned method and opens nothing; a caret click opens and closes
  and runs nothing; a main-face click closes an open list; a pin carries the glyph and hint at once; a
  method without artwork keeps the tool's glyph; the pin and the key never meet in any row of either
  language; the pin is drawn only on the hot rows and the pin stays under a pointer that reaches it;
  the pinned row keeps its fill and bar; a split cell tiles one cell with no gap; a disabled main face
  leaves the caret enabled; a caret click through the real ribbon writes no document revision.
- Verified by eye on the running app: dark and light at 100 % and 200 % (window captures of the Sketch
  row: the split cells, the pinned rectangle drawing the *from centre* glyph from `settings.json`, the
  caret wedge at rest), plus one light-200 capture with the pointer over a main face, which shows the
  wash over that face only and the caret column untinted. The pointer could not be driven reliably
  (relative `ydotool` moves against a tiling layout), so the caret-face hover, the open list and the
  pin states were verified headlessly by reading the painted rects, the image count and the text
  places; the owner's session and settings file were restored afterwards.

# X36 — a split cell is a full cell with its caret beside it

## What was measured

- `icons::ribbon_split_cell` took `RIBBON_CELL_WIDTH` (34) as the *control* width and cut the 10 pt
  caret out of it, so the main face was 24 pt; a 22 pt drawing in a 24 pt face leaves 1 pt of air on
  each side, which is what read as a cut-off glyph.
- `paint_split_cell` centred the glyph on the whole control to hide the squeeze, so a split cell's
  glyph sat about two points right of a plain cell's glyph in the same row.
- The caret is 10 x 30 pt: already past the 10 x 10 a pointer needs, so it needed no change.
- Four of the six cells of the *Sketch* row are split (line, rectangle, circle, arc), and the same
  pattern repeats on *Model* and *Furniture*: the defect met the person on every tab.

## Decisions

- `ribbon_split_cell(ui, cell_width, ...)` now allocates `cell_width + RIBBON_CARET_WIDTH`, puts the
  seam at `rect.left() + cell_width` and the glyph in the middle of the main face. The call site is
  unchanged (`RIBBON_CELL_WIDTH`), so the *cell* stays the pitch and the caret is an addition.
- `GroupSpec::columns`/`TabSpec::columns` still count tool cells, and their docs now say so: the drawn
  row is wider than that count by one caret per listed tool, and nothing sizes the row from the count.
- `theme::RIBBON_CELL_WIDTH`'s doc carries the anatomy, and its metric test asserts the split control
  is 44 pt and sits on the 4 pt grid, so the caret cannot drift back into the cell.
- ADR 0091 gained a second amendment (the first removed the fields), because the 34 x 30 cell is an
  approved metric and the split cell is where it is spent.

## Ruled out

- Shrinking the caret to 8 pt to keep the control at 34: 8 x 8 is under the 24 x 24 target rule and
  buys four points by making the list harder to open.
- Widening every cell to 44: a plain tool would carry ten points of dead air and the owner-approved
  34 x 30 pitch would be gone for every row.
- Centring the glyph on the control (the old code): it lands off the pitch of every plain cell beside
  it, which is the second half of the defect.
- Two rows or a scroll area for a wider row: ADR 0091 already answers that with a second row, and no
  tab needs one — the widest body is still inside the 640 pt minimum window, now asserted.
- A divider between the faces: unchanged from U74 — the hover and the caret column say where the
  split is, and a rule at rest would draw a second button.

## What the session checked

- Headless: the anatomy test (main face = cell, caret beside it, faces touch), the pitch test (a plain
  cell, a main face and the cell after the caret are one cell apart), the glyph test (both draw the
  same image, one cell apart), and a new body-width test over every drawn tab.
- By eye, in an offscreen shot under `target/ui-shots`: `graphite_copper-{dark,light}-opacity100-
  expanded-scale{100,200}.png`. The six *Sketch* cells read as one rhythm — pencil, caret, polyline,
  rectangle, caret, circle, caret, arc, caret — with the same glyph size and the same six points of
  air on each side of every drawing.

# X37 — an open method list owns the pointer across its whole rectangle

## What was measured

- `apps/furnicraft/src/document.rs:1990` is the only place a viewport cursor is decided, and it
  writes last: `frame()` draws the ribbon at `main.rs:822` and the viewport at `main.rs:876`, so
  `ctx.set_cursor_icon` in the viewport overwrote anything the popup wrote earlier in the pass.
- `Popup::show` returns an `InnerResponse`; its `response.rect` is the popup's own rectangle
  (measured: 300 pt wide, the 298 pt content plus the frame's 1 pt stroke on each side).
- `Chrome::covers` (`apps/furnicraft/src/side_panels.rs:233`) held the ribbon band and the two
  side panels only. The open list hangs *below* the band, so `pointer_on_chrome` was false over it
  and the armed tool's crosshair was written for the whole popup.
- Nothing in the list claimed a cursor. A row's own response is one `Sense::click()` over the row,
  but the frame's last writer wins, so per-row claims could not have decided it either.
- The defect was reproduced headlessly before the fix: the new frame test failed with
  `the list owns the pointer over the key` at 0.80 of the list width, and passed at 0.35 (the name)
  — exactly the column split the entry reported.

## Decisions

- The popup reports its rectangle as `list::Shown::rect`, the ribbon carries it in
  `RibbonOutcome::list_rect`, `Document::show_ribbon` stores it in `Shell::list_rect`, and
  `Chrome::covers` includes it. The popup *is* chrome: it is a surface drawn over the scene, so a
  press or the pointer inside it never reaches a pick, a gizmo or a border.
- The rect travels through the outcome rather than a `Toolbars` field because `Toolbars` derives
  `Eq`, and a `Rect` is not `Eq`; the outcome is also exactly "what drawing the ribbon did".
- The list claims `CursorIcon::Default` itself, once, from the popup's response rectangle. It is
  redundant with the viewport's `Default`-over-chrome branch today, and it is what makes the list
  self-sufficient if a later surface below it writes a cursor of its own.
- The list's cursor is the arrow, not a pointing hand: every chrome surface in the frame — cells,
  tabs, panels — shows the arrow, and egui sets `interact_cursor` nowhere in this program.

## Ruled out

- A per-row cursor claim: the row response does not cover the key column or the reserved pin
  column, and the last writer still wins, so three of the four columns would stay wrong.
- Making the popup a child of the ribbon band rect (a taller band): the band is also the scene's
  top and the panels' inset, so it would move the viewport and the side panels for a transient
  menu.
- Reading the popup id with `ctx.read_response` in the viewport: the shell would need the popup's
  id, which is the ribbon's private state; the rectangle is the fact both sides need.

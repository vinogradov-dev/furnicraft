# N3 — What was measured, decided and ruled out.

What was measured, decided and ruled out.

## What was measured

- `Session` carried no open product: `active_product` was the missing field ADR 0098 names, and
  `crates/engine/src/session.rs` had only the document, history and the preview overlay.
- `apps/furnicraft/src/scene_build.rs` already took `open_product: Option<Id>` (N2), and its two
  production callers in `document.rs` passed `None`.
- `Document::refresh` and `rebuild_pending` watched the revision, the visibility and the preview
  only. Openness is view state, so it needed a fourth input or opening a piece would repaint one
  frame late (or never, for a tree click).
- `crates/model/src/project/query.rs` had no placement rule, and `Project::products()` already
  walks the tree in order, so a deterministic answer needs no new index.
- The ribbon's live test table pins the exact live tools per tab, the furniture tab had one cell,
  and the icon census in `crates/ui/src/icons.rs` is a fixed count (100 before this task).
- `egui`'s `Modal` is a host `cargo xtask ui-rules` knows by owner table; the card needed its row.
- `ClassPicker::visible_rows` is family-shaped and private to its module, so the card cannot reuse
  its rows without a second function in the same module.

## Decisions

- `Session::active_product()`, `open_product(id) -> bool` and `close_product()` are view
  operations: no revision, no undo entry, no serialized field. `retain_active_product` clears the
  id after a dispatch, an undo and a redo when the product is gone or was converted to a group.
- Only a container whose class carries `level = Product` opens; a part, a group and the root leave
  the session alone. `Editor::open_product` arms the product as the creation target, so the next
  part lands inside without another gesture (ADR 0056, 0100).
- `Project::product_placement(envelope)` answers in the root frame. An empty scene is the origin;
  otherwise the box sits at the nearest product's max-X/min-Y corner on the floor and slides along
  +X until no world envelope shares a volume with it. Touching faces are clear, not an overlap, and
  a turned product is measured by the AABB of its eight corners.
- The card is window chrome (`FurniCraftApp::product_card`), like Settings and the File dialogs, so
  a tab switch never loses a half-typed frame; the ribbon reports `RibbonOutcome::open_product_card`
  and the shell draws the card and hands its draft to the active tab's editor.
- The card defaults to 600 x 560 x 720 mm and the general-product class, draws the three sides with
  the shared scrub field in whole millimetres, and searches the registry's product rows behind one
  field. `class_picker::product_rows` is the registry query; a matching family, type or variant
  keeps its relatives, exactly like the tree picker.
- Opening a product changes the envelope ink, not the revision, so `Document` keeps
  `scene_product` beside `scene_revision`; the viewport double click refreshes the scene at once,
  a tree click rebuilds on the next sync (both are one frame old at worst).
- A product row reads strong in the tree while it is open, and its context menu carries the open or
  close entry; a plain row click opens it, as ADR 0100 asks.

## Ruled out

- Storing the open product in the document: ADR 0029 and 0098 forbid it, and a saved file would then
  disagree with the workspace of the person opening it.
- Drawing the card from the editor: it would disappear with a tab switch and would need the ribbon
  to stay alive under a tab that is not active. The shell already owns the window's modals.
- Reusing `ClassPicker` as a popup on a button: the card is a form, not a property assignment, and
  the picker's popup closes on the outside click that the card's fields need.
- Setting the creation target from `active_product` inside `target.rs`: the editor already owns an
  armed container, and opening a product is the same explicit arm the tree's *Add child* uses
  (ADR 0056) instead of a second implicit rule.
- Placement against the cursor or the active view: the card is a command with no pointer, so the
  answer has to be a function of the document alone.
- A gap between the new frame and its neighbour: the envelope is a reserved space, and touching
  faces do not overlap; a gap would be an invented dimension.

# U48 — the tree's eye and padlock

the tree's eye and padlock

## What was measured

- `Editor` (`crates/ui/src/editor/mod.rs`) held `selection` and `open: HashSet<Id>`, and
  `retain_open` pruned only containers. `scene_build::evaluate` walked every child with no way to
  skip a branch, and `scene_gizmo::icons` drew cameras and lights straight from `Project`, outside
  the frame, so hiding only the frame would have left their glyphs drawn and clickable.
- `Document::refresh` rebuilt only when the project revision moved, so a session flag alone would
  never have changed the frame.
- The four `actions` icons (`visible`, `hidden`, `lock`, `unlock`) are zero-byte placeholders, so a
  toggle draws its caption at 9 pt until U46 fills the SVGs.

## Decisions

- `ViewFlags` (`crates/ui/src/view_flags.rs`) owns `hidden`, `locked` and a `visibility_revision`
  counter; `Editor` holds one and exposes `flags`, `locked_selection`, `locked_refusal`,
  `retain_flags`. The counter is what makes `Document::refresh` rebuild a hidden branch without a
  document revision.
- The set is explicit: hiding a container writes no child flag. Consumers that project the whole
  tree (`scene_gizmo`, the light rig) ask `ViewFlags::hides`, which walks the parent chain; the
  frame builder asks `is_hidden` per child, because it never descends into a hidden node.
- The lock guard sits in the shell in three places: the viewport skips the gizmo update and its
  paint, `apply_gizmo_commit` re-checks the batch, and the tree plus the palette refuse the
  destructive command ids before a dispatch. No command changed, so the document never learns about
  locks (ADR 0065).
- `Document::show_scene_tree` rebuilds right after the tree draws, so a toggle is visible in the
  same frame; `refresh` returns at once when neither revision moved.
- The row is painted by hand — one `interact` over the label rect and two 16 pt cells at the right —
  because `selectable_label` claims the whole width. The cells are reserved at rest, so a hover never
  moves the text, and a hidden or locked row keeps its toggle on screen.
- Hidden lights are skipped by the light rig as well as the frame, so hiding a light switches it
  off; a hidden camera draws no glyph and takes no click.

## Ruled out

- Expanding a hide into a flag on every descendant: unhiding the parent would then have to guess
  which children the user had hidden on their own. The ancestor walk costs one `parent_of` per level
  and keeps the user's choices.
- Storing visibility or lock in the document, with undo and a schema field: ADR 0065 rejects it, and
  `a_toggle_is_view_state_and_moves_no_document_revision` keeps it that way.
- Leaving the toggles out until U46: the row already knows how to draw a caption fallback, so U46
  only replaces the face.
- Flagging descendants in the tree too: a child of a hidden container is listed normally and the
  parent's eye explains why it is gone from the viewport.
- Keeping a hidden branch in the evaluation cache: `EvaluationCache::finish` drops every entry the
  pass did not touch, so showing it again rebuilds its meshes. Accepted and filed in `plan/debt.md`
  under `[perf]`.
